The Credit-Based System

To keep learning completely barrier-free, SkillBridge replaces cash payments with a reciprocal credit system. You teach to earn credits, and spend credits to learn.

Secured with 5-Credit Booking Escrow
HOW WE SECURE TRUST

Why Use Wallet Escrow?

Immediate 20-Credit Welcome Bonus

Every new candidate gets 20 credits instantly upon sign up. Start booking your first session right away.

Prevention of No-Shows

Tying booking to collateral ensures that candidates respect each other's time and calendar slots.

Automatic Session Completion Releases

Once your session is complete and you submit a rating, the escrow releases and routes the credits to your tutor.
