Safety & Trust Guide

To protect users from no-shows and booking abuse, SkillBridge applies an upfront collateral lock system:

Upfront Balance Lock

5 credits are temporarily locked from the learner's wallet upon confirmation.

Automatic Release

Once session completes, the 5 credits are automatically transferred to the tutor's wallet.

Cancellation Refunds

Cancellations return the credits fully to the learner's balance automatically.
